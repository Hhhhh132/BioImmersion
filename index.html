Physical and Experiential Characterisation of Biomaterials

Biomaterials are biologically-derived or bio-based materials, and due to the nature of how they are produced, a family of possible states can be produced by different biological, processing and environmental conditions. Explore that space from either direction: how it feels, or how it's made.

Illustrative demo dataset. Property values, descriptor associations and predictions in this prototype are mock/reference data for interface demonstration only — not laboratory measurements. All associations between experiential language and physical properties are represented as probabilistic and contextual, never as fixed definitions.
